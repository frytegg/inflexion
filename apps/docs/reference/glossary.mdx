## Product framing

<ParamField path="Inflexion" type="the protocol">
  A collateralized bilateral **derivatives market** on Arbitrum One (live on [Arbitrum Sepolia](/reference/deployments), chainId 421614) that lets Uniswap v3 LPs pay a fixed upfront premium to transfer the **in-range** impermanent-loss risk of a *specific* position to an underwriter, who posts collateral and is paid for taking the risk. At expiry the protocol pays the LP their realized IL, **capped at MaxIL**, trustlessly, from the underwriter's collateral.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="In-range convexity hedge" type="what this is — NOT 'IL insurance'">
  The product's true name. Entry **requires** $P_a \le P_0 \le P_b$ (an out-of-range position is rejected at creation), the payout is $\min(\text{realized\_IL},\,\text{MaxIL})$, and the cap is **load-bearing** for the no-bad-debt guarantee. Calling it "IL insurance" is both a demand risk (sophisticated LPs discount the truncated tail) and a reputational/regulatory one — every LP surface must show the payoff diagram so the cap never surprises an LP. Full treatment: [in-range convexity hedge](/concepts/in-range-convexity-hedge).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="FULL mode" type="collateral model — the launch mode">
  Fully collateralized: posted collateral $=$ MaxIL. This is the launch mode and the one carrying the no-bad-debt guarantee. See [collateral models](/protocol/collateral-models).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="PARTIAL mode" type="collateral model — roadmap">
  A future leverage dial where collateral $<$ MaxIL. Every PARTIAL parameter (floor curve, fee curve, liquidation buffer, circuit-breaker thresholds, exposure caps, first-loss size) **must** come from the calibrated risk-parameter set — hardcoding any of them is the exact failure the audit flagged. Not live.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="European / FULL lifecycle" type="settlement timing">
  Settlement happens **only at expiry** — no early exercise, no mark-to-market margin calls. See [the lifecycle](/protocol/lifecycle).
</ParamField>

## The two values and the IL math

<ParamField path="P" type="price of token0 in token1">
  The price of the volatile asset in the numéraire — e.g. ETH in USDC. Everything downstream is a function of how `P` moves.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="P0 (entry price)" type="oracle price at createSwap">
  The oracle price at `createSwap` — **not** the LP's original mint price. The hedge covers IL accruing **from creation onward**; any IL borne before that stays the LP's (entry-snapshot semantics).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="P_T (settlement price)" type="oracle price at expiry T">
  The price at expiry `T`, pinned to the Chainlink round active at `T` to kill settle-timing games. `realized_IL` is measured at `P_T`. See [price pinning](/security/price-pinning).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Pa, Pb" type="range bounds (price)">
  The position's lower (`Pa`) and upper (`Pb`) range bounds. Entry **requires** $P_a \le P_0 \le P_b$ — an out-of-range position reverts `PositionOutOfRange`. This is what makes the risk a clean *in-range* convexity exposure.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="L (liquidity)" type="Uniswap v3 liquidity of the position">
  **Read once at creation and STORED** in the `SwapRecord`; settlement uses the stored `L`, never re-read from the NFT — so an external `increaseLiquidity` cannot inflate the payout (invariant [I6](/security/invariants)).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="V_hold(T)" type="value if the LP had just held the entry tokens">
  $V_{\text{hold}} = \text{amount0}_{\text{entry}}\cdot P_T + \text{amount1}_{\text{entry}}$ — **affine** (a straight line) in `P_T`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="V_lp(T)" type="value of the LP position at P_T">
  Three regimes: in-range (mixed), below `Pa` (all token0), above `Pb` (all token1). **Strictly concave in range** — and that concavity sitting under the straight `V_hold` line is exactly what makes IL convex (and therefore priceable like an option).
</ParamField>

The loss being hedged is the gap between them:

$$
\text{realized\_IL} \;=\; \max\!\big(0,\; V_{\text{hold}}(T) - V_{\text{lp}}(T)\big)
$$

<ParamField path="realized_IL" type="the loss being hedged">
  Always computed as `V_hold > V_lp ? V_hold − V_lp : 0` — never an unchecked subtraction, so it can never go negative (invariant [I3](/security/invariants)). The swap covers IL accruing **from creation onward**; any IL borne before covering stays the LP's (entry-snapshot semantics). Full derivation: [the IL formula](/math/il-formula).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="V0" type="position value in the numéraire at entry">
  The position's value in token1 at entry, and the **capacity unit**: Path B capacity (`maxNotionalV0`) is denominated in `V0`, not collateral. A dust floor `MIN_POSITION_V0` rejects tiny positions.
</ParamField>

## MaxIL — the cap *and* the unit of risk

This is the single most important term in the protocol — see [the MaxIL concept page](/concepts/maxil) and [its derivation](/math/maxil-derivation) for the full treatment.

<ParamField path="MaxIL" type="max in-range IL of this specific position">
  $\text{MaxIL} = \max\big(\text{IL}(P_a),\, \text{IL}(P_b)\big)$ — the largest in-range IL the position can suffer. It is **pure geometry** (computable from `Pa, Pb, L, P0`), **frozen at creation**, **identical across all three durations**, and **L-independent in the fair-rate sense**. This is what makes positions **fungible to an underwriter within a market**.
</ParamField>

MaxIL wears two hats, both load-bearing:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="The load-bearing cap" icon="shield-halved">
    $\text{covered\_payoff} = \min(\text{realized\_IL},\,\text{MaxIL})$, and $\text{collateral}_{\text{FULL}} = \text{MaxIL} \ge \text{payout}$ by construction — so FULL cannot produce bad debt on **any** price path. Structural and oracle-independent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The normalization unit" icon="ruler">
    Pricing as a `% of MaxIL` makes the underwriter's return-on-collateral **range-width-independent**, removing adverse selection against wide ranges and unlocking full depth. The key pricing innovation.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<ParamField path="MaxIL is a normalization unit, NOT a risk metric" type="careful distinction">
  Two positions with identical MaxIL can carry very different risk (distance-to-edge, delta). Both the pool and MMs price the *specific geometry*, so MaxIL alone never sets the price.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Why the cap is correct, not a defect" type="design rationale">
  Beyond the range the LP is fully rotated into one asset; loss past that point is *directional* (foregone spot upside), not the *impermanent* loss being hedged. Capping is what keeps the product fully collateralized and trustless.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="MM quote is PER-MARKET, never per-NFT" type="consequence of fungibility">
  Because MaxIL makes positions fungible within a market, an MM quotes a load + a MaxIL-ratio band + capacity for a **whole market**, not a single NFT — the [SignedQuote](#the-signed-quote-path-b-eip-712) carries no `tokenId`.
</ParamField>

Reference magnitudes for a geometric-symmetric range:

| Range | MaxIL as % of V0 |
| --- | --- |
| ±5%  | ≈ 1.27% |
| ±10% | ≈ 2.56% |
| ±20% | ≈ 5.23% |
| ±50% | ≈ 13.76% |

## Pricing: FairValue, fairRate, FairPremium, σ_ref

The full pricing stack lives at [pricing stack](/protocol/pricing-stack) and [fair value](/concepts/fair-value); the closed form is on [the Φ-sum page](/math/fair-value-phi-sum).

<ParamField path="fairRate" type="fraction of MaxIL the claim is worth">
  $\text{fairRate} = \mathbb{E}_{Q}[\min(\text{IL},\,\text{MaxIL})] \,/\, \text{MaxIL}$ — the fraction of MaxIL the claim is worth under the risk-neutral measure. An **S-curve in $\sigma^2 T$** (≈0 calm/short, →1 violent/long). It carries **all** the vol/time dependence; MaxIL carries none. **Exact closed form, no fitted coefficients**: the v3 payoff is piecewise (constant / linear-in-`P` / `√P` arms split by the two cap-crossing prices), each arm integrated against the GBM density of `P_T` is a normal-CDF interval moment, so the value is a finite **Φ-sum** (≈6–10 terms). **L-independent** — depends only on $a = P_a/P_0$, $b = P_b/P_0$, $\sigma_{\text{ref}}$, `T`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="FairPremium" type="fair value of the claim">
  $\text{FairPremium} = \text{fairRate}\cdot\text{MaxIL}$ — **published on-chain** by the Stylus `FairValueOracle.fairPremium(token, a, b, duration, maxIL)`, which reads `σ_ref` via STATICCALL to the VolOracle.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="The Φ-sum is NEVER reimplemented off-chain" type="hard rule">
  A hard rule: the SDK reads the on-chain FairPremium and never approximates it. The only off-chain duplication permitted is the *load* math — never fairRate.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="σ_ref (reference volatility)" type="the only stochastic input to fairRate">
  $\sigma_{\text{ref}} = \max(\sigma_{\text{short}},\, \sigma_{\text{long}},\, \text{floor})$ — a poke-based, **time-aware EWMA of Chainlink-tick log-returns** over two horizons plus a hard floor, annualised over a 365-day year. **Never raw realized σ.** Deep page: [σ_ref](/math/sigma-ref).
</ParamField>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why max(...) and never raw realized σ — the mandatory caveat">
    Realized vol *understates* risk right before a regime change — a stale-σ jump is exactly where the book bleeds. The conservative `max` cannot fall below the slower estimate or the floor, so it never collapses to a deceptively calm number just before a jump. This caveat is **mandatory**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why poke-based">
    Chainlink rounds are irregular and reading deep history per quote is gas-prohibitive; a permissionless `poke` keeps an O(1) EWMA. `dt` is clamped to `[minSampleInterval, maxSampleInterval]`, and `poke` is a **no-op that emits no event when `dt < minSampleInterval`**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="sigmaComponents.binding — which input binds σ_ref">
    `0` = short EWMA, `1` = long EWMA, `2` = floor. This is **distinct from the load *regime*** (calm/normal/stressed). At the fresh deploy σ_ref sits at the `0.5e18` floor (fairRate ≈ 0.847 at floor vol).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Scope — what σ_ref is load-bearing for">
    σ_ref (and the FairValueOracle) is solvency-load-bearing for the **I10 cap and depositor solvency** — a wrong σ mis-prices the load and compresses NAV. It is **NOT** load-bearing for the FULL no-bad-debt invariant [I1](/security/invariants), which is structural and oracle-independent.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The load stack

The cvAMM's charge **over** fair value. The full curves live at [the load stack](/math/load-stack) and [pricing stack](/protocol/pricing-stack).

$$
\text{premium} = \text{FairPremium}\cdot\big(1 + \text{baseLoad} + \text{util\_skew} + \text{dispersion\_skew}\big)
$$
$$
\text{hard-capped at}\quad \text{FairPremium}\cdot(1 + \text{maxLoad}) \quad\text{(invariant I10)}
$$

<ParamField path="load / totalLoad" type="the multiplier over FairPremium">
  $\text{totalLoadWad} = \min(\text{baseLoad} + \text{util\_skew} + \text{dispersion\_skew},\ \text{maxLoad})$ — the [I10](/security/invariants) clamp.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="baseLoad" type="the structural volatility-risk premium">
  The structural premium over fair value, banded by σ_ref regime (calm/normal/stressed). Motivated by the **lone-writer CVaR gap**: a single position's CVaR95 sits at ~91–100% of MaxIL, so an uncharged writer is badly underpriced; diversification collapses the gap (~100% → 78.7% as N:1 → 100). The pool charges baseLoad — that gap is its reason to exist.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="util_skew" type="utilization skew">
  $\text{util\_skew}$ rises with $\text{locked}/(\text{locked} + \text{free})$ as the pool nears full commitment — flat below a knee, convex above, capped. Wires into the withdrawal-delay / locked-free run defense.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="dispersion_skew" type="concentration skew">
  Rises as outstanding coverage **clusters** in one width / moneyness / duration corner (the honest single-pair analogue of concentration risk — many bunched positions all hit MaxIL together in one move). A well-dispersed book is charged less.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Regime (calm / normal / stressed)" type="the load regime">
  The *load* regime, derived from σ_ref vs the loadParams bands. **Distinct from `sigmaComponents.binding`.**
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="maxLoad / maxLoadBps" type="the I10 ceiling">
  The [I10](/security/invariants) ceiling — the same value as a rate (`maxLoad`) or in basis points (`maxLoadBps`) on-chain.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Path-B premium" type="MM quote premium formula">
  $\text{premium}_B = \lceil \text{FairPremium}\cdot(1 + \text{loadBps}/10^4)\rceil$, capped at MaxIL, requiring $\text{loadBps} \le \text{maxLoadBps}$ ([I10](/security/invariants) on Path B). Premiums round **UP**.
</ParamField>

<Warning>
**All load / skew / σ primitives come from the calibrated risk-parameter set (the cvAMM block); none is hardcoded.**

- **Why this matters:** hardcoding any PARTIAL or load primitive is the exact failure the external audit flagged — calibrated constants drift away from the regime they were fit to, and a stale constant mis-prices risk.
- **The mitigation:** the cvAMM load block is read from the Monte-Carlo-produced parameter set, and fairRate itself has **no** calibrated coefficients at all — it is a pure closed form.
</Warning>

## The three pillars

The architecture, in full at [the three pillars](/concepts/three-pillars).

<Steps>
  <Step title="On-chain published FairValue (Pillar 1)">
    The exact closed-form Φ-sum, never reimplemented off-chain; the Stylus FairValueOracle is **production**. The single shared price both rails build on. See [fair value](/concepts/fair-value).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The cvAMM pool (Pillar 2 — Path A)">
    `ConvexityVault`, a dual-tranche ERC-4626 pooled passive underwriter that quotes algorithmically on-chain off FairPremium with inventory skews — always-on, signature-free, I10-capped. The **floor of liquidity**. See [Path A](/protocol/path-a-cvamm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="MM competition (Pillar 3 — Path B)">
    Sophisticated MMs compete via firm EIP-712 signed quotes **below** the pool, **no last-look**. The **ceiling of price**. Two load-bearing reasons: hedged MMs export short-gamma risk *out of* the system (a closed pool just circulates ETH short-gamma against itself); forward-vol MMs correct the pool's backward-looking σ_ref bias. See [Path B](/protocol/path-b-mm).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Paths and routing

The routing model, in full at [routing](/protocol/routing) and [two rails](/concepts/two-rails).

<ParamField path="Path A (cvAMM, default, signature-free)" type="createSwapPathA">
  Reads FairPremium + pool inventory, applies the I10 load stack, locks `ConvexityVault` collateral, and splits the premium across tranches. No keeper, no signed quote, no validity clock, no relayer.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Path B (MM signed quotes)" type="createSwap">
  An MM posts collateral + signs a quote below the pool; the LP can take it. Carries the full signed-quote rail (`validUntil`, `priceBandBps`, bitmap nonces, the Fork-2 band defense, [I9](/security/invariants)). The premium is **derived on-chain** from FairPremium as $\text{FairPremium}\cdot(1 + \text{loadBps})$; on Path B the premium goes **entirely to the MM** (no tranche split).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="createSwapRouted" type="the cheaper-of router">
  Routes the LP to the **cheaper of `{pool, valid MM quote}`**, both priced off the **same** on-chain FairPremium (a single VolOracle poke). The MM wins only if it **strictly** beats the pool; a tie resolves to Path A. An absent / expired / stale / over-band / over-load / zero-price MM quote **falls back to the pool — never reverts**. **Only the executed rail mutates nonce / capacity / lock** (so [I7](/security/invariants) holds).
</ParamField>

## Vaults and tranches

The vault model, in full at [vaults](/protocol/vaults) and [tranches](/concepts/tranches).

<ParamField path="ConvexityVault" type="the Path-A pooled underwriter">
  One vault per pair, backing all 9 markets with **fungible** USDC and quoting a separate price into each market — one insurer's treasury backing 9 product lines. Dual-tranche (senior/junior) from launch.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="UnderwriterVault" type="per-MM Path-B collateral">
  Per-MM collateral home: `deposited` / `locked` / `available = deposited − locked`. `lockCollateral` runs at **match** (never in the quote payload); `releaseAndDistribute` runs at **settle**.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Senior tranche" type="Tranche enum value 0">
  Base yield plus a small premium slice (`seniorPremiumShareBps`), **structurally protected from *underwriting* loss** while junior buffers — a "convexity savings account." Protected, not risk-free.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Junior tranche" type="Tranche enum value 1">
  Captures most of the load for a high APY, but is **first-loss and unhedged** — it bears the entire underwriting tail. A "pure vol-selling tranche."
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Structural senior protection" type="totalLocked ≤ juniorAssets">
  Enforced at **every** `lockCollateral`. Since every payout $\le$ its MaxIL $=$ its locked amount, $\sum \text{payouts} \le \text{totalLocked} \le \text{juniorAssets}$ — so junior absorbs all underwriting loss before senior is touched.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="sf = 0.60 (P1.13)" type="target tranche ratio, NOT a hard cap">
  The **target** senior fraction for UX/incentives — not a structural cap. Senior $P(\text{loss}) = 0$ is the **calibration** result (it holds while utilization $u \le 1 - sf$), not a structural guarantee.
</ParamField>

<ClaimB />

<ParamField path="Two guarantees, NEVER merged" type="the central distinction">
  **(A)** LPs are always paid (no bad debt in FULL — structural, code-enforced [I1](/security/invariants)); **(B)** depositors can lose principal. Both true; neither implies the other. See [no-bad-debt](/concepts/no-bad-debt).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Real P1.13 single-asset numbers" type="bare pool, u = 0.40">
  3y CAGR 122% median / 50% p10 / 247% p90; P(losing month) 26.5%; 1-in-100 month −20.1%; worst month −26.8%. Senior (sf = 0.60): P(loss) = 0 / worst 0%; junior worst −67%.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Idle-only yield (hard rule)" type="rehypothecation constraint">
  Locked collateral must stay instantly liquid — **never** routed to utilization-gated venues (Aave / Compound). Only idle/free capital may go to instantly-redeemable wrappers (sDAI / tokenized T-bills), with a hard cap and a nude-USDC buffer. See [immutability & custody](/security/immutability-custody).
</ParamField>

## The SignedQuote (Path B, EIP-712)

The full signing flow lives at [quote signing](/integrate/quote-signing).

The `SignedQuote` struct fields:

| Field | Type | What it carries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mm` | `address` | the market maker / signer |
| `marketId` | `bytes32` | the market (per-market, never per-NFT) |
| `loadBps` | `uint` | the MM's load over FairPremium |
| `minMaxILRatioBps` / `maxMaxILRatioBps` | `uint` | optional convenience filter on the MaxIL ratio |
| `quotePrice` | `uint` | the oracle price at signing — the **band anchor** |
| `priceBandBps` | `uint` | the Fork-2 band: auto-void if live oracle drifts beyond it |
| `model` | `CollateralModel` | FULL at launch |
| `partialRatioBps` | `uint` | `0` in FULL |
| `maxNotionalV0` | `uint` | capacity, denominated in V0 |
| `validUntil` | `uint` | latency clock (see below) |
| `quoteId` | `bytes32` | capacity / replay key |
| `nonce` | `uint` | Permit2-style bitmap |

<ParamField path="SIGNED_QUOTE_TYPEHASH" type="EIP-712 type hash">
  The keccak of the full type string with `loadBps` in struct order. Verification is OZ `SignatureChecker.isValidSignatureNow`, which supports EIP-1271 contract signers (e.g. a vault as signer).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="validUntil" type="latency control, default now + 8s, band [5s, 15s]">
  Protocol-enforced to **[5s, 15s]**. A *latency* control (not risk-capital), bounding how long a bearer signed quote survives in observer hands.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="priceBandBps + quotePrice (Fork-2 band)" type="oracle-anchored auto-void">
  The quote auto-voids on-chain if the live oracle drifts beyond `priceBandBps` from `quotePrice`. **Firm quotes, NO last-look** — all protection is deterministic on-chain.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Bitmap nonce (Permit2-style, word<<8 | bit)" type="selective cancel">
  Selective cancel of **one** quote, never cancel-all; batchable.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="quoteId / consumedNotional" type="capacity authority">
  `createSwap` requires $\text{consumedNotional}[\text{quoteId}] + V_0 \le \text{maxNotionalV0}$, then increments atomically (replay / double-spend defense). On-chain authoritative; the off-chain engine is advisory only.
</ParamField>

## The ten invariants

The never-break list, in full at [the invariants](/security/invariants).

| # | Name | Statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[I1](/security/invariants)** | No bad debt (FULL) | `payout ≤ collateral == MaxIL`. Qualified clause: capped payoff + locked collateral + solvent USDC + oracle/settlement liveness + no rehypothecation breach. |
| **[I2](/security/invariants)** | Cap correctness | `payout == min(realized_IL, MaxIL)`. |
| **[I3](/security/invariants)** | Non-negativity / no underflow | `realized_IL = V_hold > V_lp ? V_hold − V_lp : 0` — never an unchecked subtraction. |
| **[I4](/security/invariants)** | LP never profits | `V_lp ≥ V_hold ⟹ payout == 0`. A hedge, not a lottery. |
| **[I5](/security/invariants)** | Vault solvency | `locked ≤ deposited` per MM (and for the pool); `Σ locked` fully backed. |
| **[I6](/security/invariants)** | Liquidity immutability | settlement uses `L` stored at creation, never re-read; `increaseLiquidity` cannot inflate payout. |
| **[I7](/security/invariants)** | Capacity authority | `consumedNotional[quoteId] ≤ maxNotionalV0`; a cancelled bitmap-nonce bit cannot fill. |
| **[I8](/security/invariants)** | Settlement liveness (Fork 1) | `settle()` always succeeds within `expiry + LIVENESS_WINDOW + MAX_STALENESS + GRACE_PERIOD`. |
| **[I9](/security/invariants)** | Band enforcement (Fork 2) | `createSwap` reverts iff `absBps(P_live, quote.quotePrice) > quote.priceBandBps` (Path B only). |
| **[I10](/security/invariants)** | Price cap (launch) | `premium ≤ FairPremium · (1 + maxLoadBps)` on **both** paths — by construction, upstream of settle. Does not touch settle / MaxIL / I1–I9. |

## The two forks

The two audit-resolved deadlock/pickoff classes, in full at [the forks](/security/forks).

<ParamField path="Fork 1 — oracle settlement deadlock" type="resolved (Option B)">
  The v3.1 hard-TWAP gate could permanently lock funds. Replaced by Chainlink round-at-T pinning + a lone-spike check + advisory-only TWAP + a 24h liveness backstop, so settle can never deadlock ([I8](/security/invariants)).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Fork 2 — no-last-look quote pickoff" type="resolved (Option B, Path B only)">
  A bearer signed quote could be picked off after an oracle gap. Resolved by the **oracle-anchored price band** (auto-void if drift &gt; `priceBandBps`) + short `validUntil` + on-chain selective nonce invalidation — all deterministic, **NOT** last-look ([I9](/security/invariants)).
</ParamField>

## The data moat (five behavioral signals)

The first public view into the **microstructure of the DeFi LP volatility-risk premium** — non-circular, actor-driven. In full at [the five signals](/moat/five-signals) and [circular vs behavioral](/moat/circular-vs-behavioral).

<ParamField path="Signal 1 — Realized clearing LOAD over a transparent σ_ref" type="non-circular (the structural baseline)">
  Bucketed by `width × distance-to-edge × duration` (cap-bound fills excluded). Pool load = mechanical baseline (`SwapPriced.totalLoadWad`); MM load (`QuoteFilled.loadBps`) = the behavioral signal. The circular "invert fairRate for an implied-vol surface" claim is **DROPPED** — charged/MaxIL = $\text{fairRate}(\sigma_{\text{ref}})\cdot(1+\text{load})$ only recovers our own σ_ref + dealer load. See [circular vs behavioral](/moat/circular-vs-behavioral).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Signal 2 — Pool-vs-MM load spread + MM win-rate" type="behavioral (a forward-vol read)">
  The MM prices implied/forward vol; the pool prices backward-looking σ_ref. The **spread** between them is the forward-vol read. Dynamic with ≥3 MMs.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Signal 3 — Term structure of convexity" type="behavioral (the MM slope)">
  The MM `loadBps` slope across 7/30/90d per range. The Path-A load is duration-independent/mechanical, so the *slope* is the MM signal.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Signal 4 — Moneyness / demand skew" type="behavioral (realized + latent)">
  The realized on-chain half **plus** the latent/unfilled half via off-chain engine telemetry (`DEMAND_LOG`). See [telemetry](/moat/telemetry).
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Signal 5 — Net convexity / gamma supply" type="behavioral (the active-set aggregate)">
  Off-chain Greeks summed over the active set (the GreeksEngine over the subgraph). See [greeks & hedge](/sdk/greeks-hedge).
</ParamField>

<Warning>
**Honest framing of the moat (mandatory).**

- **The limit:** the clearing load is contaminated — it bundles liquidity, smart-contract risk, capital-lock, and inventory-skew premia — and the dynamic/latent halves are thin until MM and flow volume grow; the on-chain half (signals 1/2/3/5) begins only at the single subgraph redeploy.
- **Why:** these are real-world microstructure quantities that mature with participation, not synthetic data we can backfill.
- **The mitigation / path-forward:** the value is in the *architecture* of the moat. The **structures exist day-one**; you **trade the SPREAD, not the level** (the spread cancels the shared contamination); and the latent halves accrue with volume.
</Warning>

## The qualified no-bad-debt clause

Never state no-bad-debt unqualified. The full clause:

> No-bad-debt is exact **only** under: FULL collateralization + capped payoff + solvent USDC + oracle/settlement liveness + no rehypothecation breach.
