<Warning>
**LPs are always paid with no bad debt in FULL mode — qualified: capped payoff + solvent USDC + oracle/settlement liveness.**

The no-bad-debt guarantee is **exact only** under the full clause:

- **FULL collateralization** — the underwriter has posted exactly `MaxIL` per swap, locked for the life of the position.
- **Capped payoff** — the payout is `min(realized_IL, MaxIL)`. The cap is load-bearing: it is what makes `payout ≤ collateral` hold by construction.
- **Solvent USDC** — the collateral numeraire (`dUSDC`) holds its peg and remains redeemable.
- **Oracle / settlement liveness** — the price oracle stays live and `settle()` runs within the protocol's settlement window.
- **No rehypothecation breach** — locked collateral stays instantly liquid; it is never routed to utilization-gated venues.

Stated without these qualifiers, the guarantee is **not** correct.
</Warning>
