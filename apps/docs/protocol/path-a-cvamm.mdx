Path A is the **cvAMM** — a pooled, passive, always-quoting underwriter that lives
entirely on-chain. An LP calls `createSwapPathA` and is
priced and protected in a single transaction with **no market maker, no signed quote,
no validity clock, and no relayer**. The counterparty is the dual-tranche
[`ConvexityVault`](/protocol/vaults), which locks USDC collateral equal to
[MaxIL](/concepts/maxil) and is paid the premium for taking the risk.

This is **Pillar 2** of the [three pillars](/concepts/three-pillars): the cvAMM is the
**floor of liquidity**. Because it always quotes a code-capped fair price with no MM
present, there is no cold-start problem — an LP can always be priced and protected.
Path B (the [MM rail](/protocol/path-b-mm)) is the ceiling of price; it can only ever
undercut this floor, never replace it.

## What `createSwapPathA` does

`createSwapPathA` is one of three entry points into a single settlement core (the
others are [`createSwap`](/protocol/path-b-mm) and
[`createSwapRouted`](/protocol/routing)). It shares the same `view` read prologue
(`_prepareSwap`) as the other two, prices the swap on the
pool's own state, then locks, pays, and custodies in one Checks-Effects-Interactions
sweep.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wiring & market gate">
    Requires the cvAMM is wired (`convexityVault != 0`) and the market is both `active`
    and `cvammEnabled` — Path A markets must be explicitly opted in via `setCvammEnabled`.
    This is the on/off switch for the pool rail per market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shared read prologue (in-range gate)">
    `_prepareSwap` verifies `ownerOf(tokenId) == msg.sender`, derives the `marketId` from
    the live on-chain `token0/token1/fee`, reads `[Pa, Pb]` from the position ticks, pins
    `P0` to Chainlink **on-chain** (never caller-supplied), and enforces the convexity
    gate `Pa ≤ P0 ≤ Pb` else `PositionOutOfRange`. It
    computes `MaxIL`, snapshots the entry amounts `(a0, a1)`, and the position value
    `V0`. The liquidity `L` is captured here (`g.liquidity`) and
    stored at execution — settlement uses the **stored** `L`, never a re-read
    ([I6](/security/invariants)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fair value (one poke, one Φ-sum)">
    `_fairPremium` converts geometry to ratios
    `a = Pa/P0`, `b = Pb/P0`, **pokes the [VolOracle](/protocol/oracles)** to refresh
    `σ_ref` (permissionless, no-op if too soon), and reads
    `FairValueOracle.fairPremium(...)` → `(FairPremium, fairRateWad, sigmaRefWad)`. This
    is the [exact closed-form Φ-sum](/math/fair-value-phi-sum) — the production Stylus
    pricer, never reimplemented off-chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Price = FairPremium + the on-chain load stack">
    `_pricePathA` applies the I10-clamped
    [load stack](/math/load-stack) on top of `FairPremium`, then caps the charged premium
    at MaxIL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dust guards & slippage">
    Like all three entry points, Path A enforces `MIN_POSITION_V0 = $100` and
    `MIN_PREMIUM = $1` (block dust swaps that grief capacity and close the integer-division
    free-coverage edge), plus a final `maxPremium` LP slippage
    guard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute — lock, pay, custody (CEI)">
    `_executePathA` performs all state changes and external
    calls. See the next section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Execution: `_executePathA`

This is the only place Path-A state mutates. It follows strict Checks-Effects-Interactions
([CEI](/security/cei-reentrancy)):

1. **Lock collateral.** `convexityVault.lockCollateral(marketId, maxIL)` — the pool checks
   that `free ≥ maxIL` (`InsufficientFree`) **and** that the senior tranche stays protected,
   `totalLocked + maxIL ≤ juniorAssets` (`SeniorProtectionBreached`). Collateral locked is
   **exactly MaxIL** (FULL mode).
2. **Allocate the swap id.** `nextSwapId++`.
3. **Write the record.** `SwapRecord{ mm: address(convexityVault), collateral: maxIL,
   liquidity: g.liquidity (stored once — I6), ... }`. The counterparty field is named `mm`
   and is set to the vault address; `settle` later infers the rail from
   `s.mm == address(convexityVault)`.
4. **Pull the premium** in USDC from the LP.
5. **Take the NFT** into the [`ILVault`](/protocol/vaults) for custody.
6. **Split the premium** (next section).

There is **no keeper, no signed quote, no validity clock, no relayer** anywhere in this
path. Everything is synchronous and on-chain.

## The on-chain load stack

Path A computes its load **on-chain** from the pool's own state (Path B carries a single
`loadBps` instead). The premium is `FairPremium` scaled up by a load that is the sum of
three components, then hard-clamped:

$$
\text{premium}_A \;=\; \left\lceil\, \text{FairPremium}\cdot\bigl(1 + \text{totalLoad}\bigr) \,\right\rceil,
\qquad
\text{totalLoad} \;=\; \min\!\bigl(\underbrace{\text{baseLoad} + \text{util\_skew} + \text{disp\_skew}}_{\text{raw load}},\ \text{maxLoad}\bigr)
$$

The outer `min(..., maxLoad)` is the [I10 clamp](/security/invariants)
(`CvammPricing.totalLoadWad`); the round-up is F-#8
(`CvammPricing.premiumFromLoad`). All quantities are
WAD-scaled — `1e18 = 1.0 = 100% load`, `1 bps = 1e14 WAD`.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="baseLoad — the structural volatility-risk premium" icon="layer-group">
    The structural premium over fair value, keyed by the `σ_ref` regime (calm / normal /
    stressed bands) — `CvammPricing.baseLoadWad`. It is
    motivated by the lone-writer CVaR gap (~91–100% of MaxIL for a single writer) that
    diversification collapses (~78.7% per-contract as N→100). **That gap is the pool's
    reason to exist.**
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="util_skew(u) — capacity pricing, u = locked / total" icon="gauge-high">
    Flat below a knee, convex above, capped — `CvammPricing.utilSkewWad`.
    It rises as the pool nears full commitment, pricing
    marginal capacity **up before over-commitment**. This wires directly into the
    [run defense](/protocol/vaults): the same locked/free accounting that prices capacity
    also blocks a withdrawal run.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="disp_skew(h) — concentration pricing, h = coverage HHI" icon="diagram-project">
    The honest single-pair concentration analogue: many positions bunched at one range edge
    all hit MaxIL together. Convex, capped — `CvammPricing.dispSkewWad`.
    The Herfindahl `h` is computed **O(1)** from an incremental
    `Σ lockedByMarket²` accumulator (`ConvexityVault.concentrationWad`) — no loop over
    positions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
**No primitive is hardcoded.** The `baseLoad` regimes, both skew curves
(knee / slope / power / cap), and `maxLoadBps` are read from the calibrated risk-parameter
set (cvAMM block) via `LoadParams` (set by the owner through
`InflexionCore.setLoadParams`). `fairRate` itself has **no**
calibrated coefficients — it is the exact closed form. Hardcoding any load / skew / σ
primitive is the exact failure the external audit flagged.
</Note>

### The I10 cap is by construction

<Warning>
**Limit → Why → Mitigation: can the pool overcharge?**

- **Limit (apparent):** a pooled algorithmic underwriter that quotes itself could, in
  principle, mark the price arbitrarily high.
- **Why it cannot here:** the raw load is `baseLoad + util_skew + disp_skew`, but the
  charged load is `min(raw, maxLoad)`. The clamp is a deterministic arithmetic operation
  in the pricing (READ) phase, strictly **upstream of `settle`** — not a policy or a trust
  assumption.
- **Mitigation (in code):** `premium ≤ FairPremium · (1 + maxLoad)`
  ([I10](/security/invariants)), and both paths additionally cap
  the charged premium at MaxIL — *never charge more than the maximum possible payout*.
  I10 is orthogonal to the [no-bad-debt](/concepts/no-bad-debt)
  invariant I1: it never touches `settle`, the MaxIL formula, or the payout cap.
</Warning>

## The premium split — 99% to the pool, 1% to the treasury

When the premium lands, `_executePathA` splits it:

| Cut | Share | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `poolCut` | **99%** | accrued into the `ConvexityVault` via `accruePremium` |
| `treasuryCut` | **1%** | transferred to the protocol treasury |

The 99% pool cut is then split **across tranches** inside the vault by
`accruePremium`:

$$
\text{toSenior} = \text{poolCut}\cdot\frac{\text{seniorPremiumShareBps}}{10^4},
\qquad
\text{toJunior} = \text{poolCut} - \text{toSenior}
$$

So the senior tranche behaves like a low-yield "convexity savings account" and the junior
tranche is the high-APY vol-seller. `seniorPremiumShareBps` is immutable, sourced from
the calibrated risk-parameter set. This tranche split is **unique to
Path A** — Path B's split is a flat 99% to the MM / 1% to the treasury, with no tranche
allocation.

<Tip>
The premium split is **not** part of the settlement guarantee. Neither the tranche split
nor the `totalLocked ≤ juniorAssets` senior-protection constraint affects I10 or
[`settle`](/protocol/lifecycle) semantics. The premium is the underwriter's
reward; the [no-bad-debt clause](/concepts/no-bad-debt) concerns only the LP's payout, paid
from locked collateral.
</Tip>

## Signature-free, always available — the floor of liquidity

Path A is the answer to three cold-start failure modes that kill quote-driven markets:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="No price (cold-start)" icon="snowflake">
    A pure MM market has **no price until an MM shows up**. The cvAMM always quotes a
    code-capped fair price for any in-range position in any enabled market.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Overcharge" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    A monopolist early MM could mark prices up. The pool's price is **clamped in code** by
    I10 — it cannot exceed `FairPremium · (1 + maxLoad)`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Liveness dependency" icon="plug-circle-xmark">
    Path A needs **no off-chain engine**. The engine [cannot censor Path A at all](/security/trust-model)
    because Path A does not use it — there is nothing to censor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Depositor viability" icon="users">
    The dual-tranche design gives depositors a choice: a senior "savings account" or a
    junior vol-seller. See [tranches](/concepts/tranches).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

This is what "floor of liquidity" means precisely: **an LP can always get a code-capped
fair quote on Path A**, regardless of whether any MM is online, whether the off-chain
quote book is live, or whether the [subgraph](/integrate/subgraph) is deployed. The MM
rail is a *price-improvement layer* on top of this floor — through
[`createSwapRouted`](/protocol/routing), an MM wins only by **strictly beating** the
pool, and a bad MM quote silently falls back to Path A without ever reverting.

## Proven live

The 2026-06-05 redeploy ran a real create→settle on Path A against a 300s market:

- **Path A (swap #1):** V0 **\$270,531.28**, MaxIL **\$1,669.24**, premium **\$9.70 (0.58% of
  MaxIL)**, realized IL **\$148.64**, payout **\$148.64** — locked and settled entirely from
  the `ConvexityVault`. The LP received the payout; the pool kept `collateral − payout`
  (the residual MaxIL) as profit on top of the premium.

In the same lifecycle, `createSwapRouted` (swap #2) compared `premiumA_cvamm $13.80`
against an MM's `premiumB_mm $8.93` and routed to the MM — the pool is the floor and the
MM the ceiling. See [routing](/protocol/routing) for that path.
