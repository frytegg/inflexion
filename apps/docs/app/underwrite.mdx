The `/underwrite` page is the **Path-B market-maker rail** in the live app. It is where
you, an MM, post collateral, build and **sign a firm EIP-712 quote**, and publish it to
the off-chain engine so an LP can fill it on-chain. The whole flow rests on one idea: an
MM quote is **per-market, never per-NFT** — because [MaxIL](/concepts/maxil) is the
fungible unit of risk.

You are doing the opposite of the [LP on `/protect`](/app/protect): they pay a fixed
premium to shed in-range IL convexity; you collect that premium for taking it. You are
**short convexity, paid the premium for it**, and your capital is at risk in a way the
LP's is not.

<Warning>
**Your capital is NOT guaranteed. You are short convexity and paid the premium for
it.** The no-bad-debt guarantee on [`/protect`](/app/protect) is for the protected LP —
**not for you**. On a fill the protocol locks your own collateral at MaxIL and pays the
LP `min(realized_IL, MaxIL)` from it at settlement. The page header states this
verbatim; the full disclosure is at the bottom of this page. This is a **separate
claim** from the LP guarantee — the two are never merged.
</Warning>

## Section 1 — Post collateral (UnderwriterVault)

Your collateral lives in the per-MM **`UnderwriterVault`** — separate from the Path-A
[`ConvexityVault`](/protocol/path-a-cvamm) that pooled depositors fund. A quote cannot
be filled against collateral you have not posted: at fill the contract requires
`availableBalance(mm) ≥ maxIL` of the position, else `MMUndercollateralised`.

The `CollateralPanel` reads `mm.getMmCollateral(address)` every 12s and shows three
numbers:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Deposited** | total dUSDC you have posted |
| **Locked** (amber) | $\sum \text{MaxIL}$ across your active fills — your committed collateral |
| **Available to quote** (teal) | `deposited − locked` — what new quotes can draw on |

This is [invariant I5](/security/invariants): `locked ≤ deposited`, by construction.
Locked collateral is never re-routed; it stays instantly liquid to pay LPs at
settlement.

### Deposit is two transactions — and there is no SDK helper for it

Collateral deposit/withdraw is a **separate, direct on-chain flow**. The SDK
deliberately exposes the ABI + address but **no `MmClient` method for collateral** — it
does not invent one — so the page calls the documented `UnderwriterVault` functions
directly via the wallet client.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve (only if your allowance is short)">
    ERC-20 `approve(UnderwriterVault, amount)` on dUSDC. The spender is the
    **UnderwriterVault** — not InflexionCore (the LP-premium spender) and not the
    ConvexityVault (the depositor spender). Three different spenders; this is the
    recurring gotcha.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    `UnderwriterVault.deposit(amount)`. A **Max** button fills your wallet balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```ts UnderwriterVault deposit (direct, via wagmi walletClient)
import { core, tokens } from '@inflexion/sdk'
import { erc20Abi } from 'viem'

const amount = 50_000_000_000n // 50,000 dUSDC, 6-dec raw

// 1. Approve only if the current allowance is short.
const allowance = await publicClient.readContract({
  address: tokens.demoUsdc,
  abi: erc20Abi,
  functionName: 'allowance',
  args: [account, core.underwriterVault], // spender == UnderwriterVault
})
if (allowance < amount) {
  await walletClient.writeContract({
    address: tokens.demoUsdc,
    abi: erc20Abi,
    functionName: 'approve',
    args: [core.underwriterVault, amount],
  })
}

// 2. Deposit into your per-MM vault.
await walletClient.writeContract({
  address: core.underwriterVault,
  abi: underwriterVaultAbi, // exported from @inflexion/sdk
  functionName: 'deposit',
  args: [amount],
})
```

**Withdraw** is a single tx — `UnderwriterVault.withdraw(amount)` — and only the
**available** (un-locked) portion is withdrawable; the locked part is committed to open
fills.

<Warning>
**Collateral must stay instantly liquid — never lock it elsewhere.**

- **The rule:** the page's deposit hint says exactly this. It is a protocol invariant,
  not a UI nicety.
- **Why:** your locked collateral is the *only* source the LP is paid from at
  settlement. If it were routed to a utilization-gated venue (Aave / Compound) it could
  be un-redeemable at the exact moment it is needed, breaking the
  [no-bad-debt guarantee](/concepts/no-bad-debt) the LP relies on.
- **The path forward:** idle (un-locked) capital only may ever earn yield, and only via
  instantly-redeemable wrappers (sDAI / tokenized T-bills). Locked collateral stays put.
  See [collateral models](/protocol/collateral-models).
</Warning>

## Section 2 — Build & sign a firm quote (PER-MARKET, no tokenId)

This is the conceptual heart of the page (`QuoteBuilder`). **A quote is per-market, not
per-position.** You pick a market and express a single appetite; the quote then fills
**any in-range position in that market** whose MaxIL/V0 ratio falls in your band, up to
your capacity. The contract checks the band at fill.

<Info>
**There is no `tokenId` anywhere in a quote.** The `SignedQuote` struct carries 13
fields and not one of them is a position id. This is the entire design, not an
omission — see [why fungibility makes it per-market](#why-per-market-not-per-nft).
</Info>

### The inputs you set

The builder takes six inputs:

<ParamField path="Market" type="dropdown — one of 9">
  Picks the `marketId` over all 9 markets (3 fee tiers × 3 durations) and sets the
  `oracleToken` anchor your `quotePrice` is read against. One quote binds to exactly one
  market.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Min / Max MaxIL ratio (bps)" type="0 → 10000">
  Your **appetite band**: the MaxIL/V0 ratios you are willing to write. `10000` (=
  100%) means "cover any ratio". This is *which positions you cover*, never a price
  input.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Your load (bps)" type="≤ maxLoadBps AND < pool load" required>
  Your markup over the on-chain `FairPremium`. It must satisfy **both** guards:
  `loadBps ≤ maxLoadBps` ([I10](/security/invariants)) **and** strictly below the live
  pool load, or your quote will never win the route.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Price band (bps)" type="25 → 500 on-chain">
  The [Fork-2](/security/forks) band around your signed `quotePrice`. An LP can only
  fill your quote while the live oracle price stays within ± this band of your anchor.
  This is your firm-quote, no-last-look protection.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Max notional (V0)" type="uint128, numéraire raw">
  Your capacity cap for this quote ([I7](/security/invariants)). The on-chain ledger
  `consumedNotional[quoteId]` enforces it across every fill.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="Valid for (s)" type="bound to now + [5,15]s on-chain">
  The validity window. On-chain `secondsAhead` is bound to `[5s, 15s]` — a short leakage
  window for a bearer signature.
</ParamField>

The premium an LP ultimately pays is **derived from your load over the published fair
value** — you never stream a raw premium:

$$
\text{premium}_B \;=\; \left\lceil \text{FairPremium}\cdot\Bigl(1 + \tfrac{\text{loadBps}}{10^4}\Bigr) \right\rceil, \qquad \text{capped at MaxIL}
$$

where `FairPremium = fairRate · MaxIL` is the closed-form
[Φ-sum](/math/fair-value-phi-sum) read on-chain, never reimplemented.

### The live reference cards (right side)

While you set inputs, four cards stream live state so you can price competitively:

  </Accordion>

### Why per-market, not per-NFT {#why-per-market-not-per-nft}

The absence of `tokenId` is the direct consequence of [MaxIL](/concepts/maxil) being
the **fungible unit of risk**. Because MaxIL is pure geometry, frozen at creation,
identical across durations, and L-independent in the fair-rate sense, every position in
a market becomes **interchangeable to you as an underwriter**. You do not need to see a
specific NFT to price it — you express one appetite, once, and the contract enforces it
per-fill.

So your quote carries a triple:

$$
\text{quote} \;=\; \bigl(\underbrace{\text{loadBps}}_{\text{price}},\; \underbrace{[\text{minMaxILRatioBps},\, \text{maxMaxILRatioBps}]}_{\text{appetite}},\; \underbrace{\text{maxNotionalV0}}_{\text{capacity}}\bigr)
$$

and **any** LP position in that market whose ratio falls in your band can fill it. The
band is the only place the LP's specific geometry enters the gate, and the contract
computes it **from the LP's own position**, not from your quote:

$$
\text{ratioBps} \;=\; \frac{\text{maxIL}\cdot 10^4}{V_0}, \qquad \text{require}\;\; \text{ratioBps} \in [\text{minMaxILRatioBps},\, \text{maxMaxILRatioBps}]
$$

<Tip>
The MaxIL-ratio band is a **convenience appetite filter, not an adverse-selection
fix**. Every v3 position parameter is public on-chain via `positions(tokenId)` — both
rails read the exact geometry, so there is no information asymmetry to defend against.
The band just lets you say "I only write 2%–7% MaxIL/V0 widths" once, and every
conforming position is fungible to you. See [Path B in depth](/protocol/path-b-mm).
</Tip>

### Guard banners — you cannot sign a losing quote

Before the Sign button activates, the builder shows two guard banners:

- an **I10 banner** if `loadBps > maxLoadBps`;
- a **"does not undercut the live pool" banner** if `loadBps >= poolBps` — *"A quote at
  or above the pool floor will not win the route."*

The Sign button is gated on `canSign` = valid inputs **AND** I10 OK **AND** below-pool
**AND** wallet connected. This mirrors the same guards `mm.signQuote` enforces
server-side.

### Signing — browser EIP-712, no private key in the browser

On click, the page does **not** trust the values you set a moment ago — it re-reads
fresh state and re-enforces the guards right before signing:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Re-read & re-enforce I10 + below-pool">
    Fetch fresh `maxLoadBps` and pool load, re-check both guards. A stale-tab quote that
    has drifted above the pool is rejected at the last instant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the fresh oracle price for quotePrice">
    The signed `quotePrice` is the *current* oracle price, not a cached one. This is the
    Fork-2 band anchor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the SignedQuote">
    `model = FULL (0)`, `partialRatioBps = 0`, a random `quoteId`, and a bitmap
    `nonce = encodeNonce(word, bit)`. PARTIAL is enum-only and reverts — FULL is the
    only mode in v1.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign with the connected wallet">
    `walletClient.signTypedData` over the domain
    `{ 'Inflexion', '1', 421614, core.inflexionCore }`, `SignedQuoteTypes`, primaryType
    `SignedQuote`. No key ever touches the browser JS — your wallet signs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The 13-field struct order **must match `InflexionCore.SIGNED_QUOTE_TYPEHASH` exactly**
or the on-chain `createSwap` / `createSwapRouted` rejects the signature. The canonical
browser pattern, mirroring what the page does:

```ts browser quote signing (wagmi) — mirrors /underwrite
import { SignedQuoteTypes, quoteDomain, core, CHAIN_ID, encodeNonce } from '@inflexion/sdk'

// 1. Enforce I10 + below-pool BEFORE signing (same guards as mm.signQuote).
const params = await mm.getLoadParams()
if (quote.loadBps > params.maxLoadBps) throw new Error('i10-exceeded')

const pool = await mm.getPoolLoadToBeat(quote.marketId)
if (pool.available && quote.loadBps >= Number(pool.totalLoadBps)) {
  throw new Error('not-below-pool') // a quote at/above the floor never wins the route
}

// 2. Read the FRESH oracle price as the Fork-2 band anchor.
//    (the page reads OracleManager.getPrice(oracleToken) here)

// 3. Build the PER-MARKET quote — note: NO tokenId.
const quote = {
  mm: account,
  marketId,                                  // one of the 9 markets
  loadBps: Number(pool.totalLoadBps) - 50,   // undercut the pool by 50 bps
  minMaxILRatioBps: 0,
  maxMaxILRatioBps: 10_000,                   // cover any MaxIL/V0 ratio
  quotePrice: liveOraclePrice,                // bigint — the fresh oracle anchor
  priceBandBps: 100,                          // ±1% Fork-2 band
  model: 0,                                    // CollateralModel.FULL
  partialRatioBps: 0,
  maxNotionalV0: 1_000_000_000_000n,          // capacity (numéraire raw, V0 units)
  validUntil: BigInt(Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000) + 8),
  quoteId: ('0x' + crypto.randomUUID().replace(/-/g, '').padEnd(64, '0')) as `0x${string}`,
  nonce: encodeNonce(0n, 0),                   // bitmap (word<<8)|bit — selective cancel
}

// 4. Sign with the connected wallet (EIP-712, no raw key in the browser).
const signature = await walletClient.signTypedData({
  account: walletClient.account,
  domain: quoteDomain(CHAIN_ID, core.inflexionCore), // { 'Inflexion','1',421614, core }
  types: SignedQuoteTypes,
  primaryType: 'SignedQuote',
  message: quote,                              // bigints for uint128 / uint64 / uint256
})
const envelope = { quote, signature }          // → publish to the engine, or hand to an LP
```

### Publish to the engine

The signed envelope is POSTed to `${NEXT_PUBLIC_ENGINE_URL}/quote` as a `QuoteWire` —
bigints serialized to decimal strings via `encodeQuote`. The signed-envelope card
prints `loadBps`, band, `quotePrice`, `maxNotionalV0`, `validUntil` (local time), model,
`quoteId`, and `nonce`.

```bash publish a signed quote to the engine
curl -X POST "$ENGINE_URL/quote" \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "quote": {
      "mm": "0x...",
      "marketId": "0x67c4bee1...6479d69",
      "loadBps": 850,
      "minMaxILRatioBps": 0,
      "maxMaxILRatioBps": 10000,
      "quotePrice": "...",
      "priceBandBps": 100,
      "model": 0,
      "partialRatioBps": 0,
      "maxNotionalV0": "1000000000000",
      "validUntil": "...",
      "quoteId": "0x...",
      "nonce": "0"
    },
    "signature": "0x..."
  }'
```

### Engine offline — you still sign {#engine-offline-you-still-sign}

<Warning>
**If `NEXT_PUBLIC_ENGINE_URL` is unset, the quote cannot be published — but signing
still works and nothing crashes.**

- **Limit:** without the engine relay, the page cannot POST your quote to an LP-facing
  book, so no one can discover and fill it through the app.
- **Why:** Path B is the only rail that needs an off-chain quote book; Path A (the
  pool) is signature-free and always-on, so the absence of the engine never affects an
  LP's ability to get protection.
- **Mitigation:** the page shows **"engine not configured"** and renders the **exact
  wire envelope** you would hand the engine WS — or that an LP could pass directly to
  `lp.buyProtection`. Because EIP-712 verification is on-chain, any LP holding your valid
  signed quote can call `createSwap` directly, with no engine in the loop. The relay is a
  convenience, never a trust dependency. See the [trust model](/security/trust-model).
</Warning>

## Section 3 — Book, fills, and cancel

`BookAndFills` is your live position view.

### Your book — active fills

`mm.getBook(address)` every 15s shows **Exposure ($\sum V_0$)** and **Locked ($\sum
\text{MaxIL}$ == your committed collateral, amber)**, plus a per-swap table of swap / V0
/ MaxIL / premium / expiry. Discovery is an **on-chain swap scan** (no subgraph) — so
aggregate exposure is exact, today.

### Cancel quote nonces

Paste space/comma-separated decimal nonces (`(word<<8)|bit`) and `mm.cancelNonces`
**burns the bitmap bit so that quote can never fill again**. This is the on-chain
backstop to the engine's cancel: even if the relay still has the bytes, a cancelled bit
is dead on-chain instantly.

```ts cancel one quote's nonce on-chain
import { encodeNonce } from '@inflexion/sdk'

// Cancel exactly the quote signed with nonce (word=0, bit=0) — never cancel-all.
const txHash = await mm.cancelNonces([encodeNonce(0n, 0)])
```

### Recent fills — coarse attribution (and why)

The `FillsFeed` (`mm.watchFills`) polls `SwapCreated` logs filtered by your `mm`
address.

<Warning>
**Fill attribution is coarse today — you see *that* you filled, not *which quote*
filled.**

- **Limit:** the live `SwapCreated` event carries the `mm` address but **not** the
  `quoteId` or `nonce`, so a fill cannot be joined back to a specific signed quote. The
  badge in the UI says **"coarse"**.
- **Why:** `isNonceUsed(mm, nonce)` is also set on *both* fill and cancel, so it cannot
  by itself distinguish a fill from a cancellation.
- **Mitigation / path forward:** the deploy **already emits** the precise `QuoteFilled`
  event (`swapId, mm, quoteId, nonce, loadBps`) on-chain since block 274081134. Precise
  per-quote attribution becomes available the moment the subgraph indexes it — it
  degrades to a typed *pending* state in the meantime, never a fake number. Your
  aggregate exposure and locked collateral are exact today regardless.
</Warning>

This is the rail end to end: you win by undercutting the pool, your own collateral backs
