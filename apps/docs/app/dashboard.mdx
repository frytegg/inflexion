The Dashboard is the single place a connected wallet sees everything it touches in
Inflexion, across all three roles at once:

1. **Protections (LP, [claim A](/concepts/no-bad-debt))** — every swap where you are the
   protected LP, with a per-card **Settle** button at expiry.
2. **Deposits (depositor, [claim B](/concepts/tranches))** — your senior and junior NAV
   in the pooled cvAMM vault, with inline **request-withdrawal** and **withdraw**.
3. **MM Book (market maker, [claim B](/concepts/tranches))** — the swaps you underwrite on
   [Path B](/protocol/path-b-mm), plus your collateral usage in the `UnderwriterVault`.

Unlike [`/markets`](/app/markets) or [`/data`](/app/data), the Dashboard is **per-user**
and **write-capable**: it is where the asymmetric actions happen — collect an LP payout,
queue a tranche exit. It is built on a **bounded on-chain swap scan** rather than the
subgraph, so it is fully live today within that bound.

<Note>
**Reads degrade, never throw.** Discovery walks the chain directly (no subgraph yet); any
read that can't resolve — a reverting oracle, an absent record, the not-yet-indexed
subgraph — renders a typed `PendingNote` with its reason, never an error. "Pending" is a deliberate trust signal, not a bug.
See [degradation](/sdk/degradation) and [status & caveats](/reference/status-caveats).
</Note>

## How discovery works — one bounded scan, three slices

There is no subgraph indexing events yet, so the page cannot ask "give me all of this
user's swaps." Instead it enumerates the global swap table directly and filters client-side.

The LP slice reads `nextSwapId` to bound the walk, then iterates **downward** from the
most recent swap for up to `SCAN_LIMIT = 400` ids:

```ts
const next = await read('nextSwapId')          // swap ids are 1-indexed
const last = next > 0n ? next - 1n : 0n
const lo   = last > 400n ? last - 400n + 1n : 1n // bounded window

for (let id = last; id >= lo && id >= 1n; id--) {
  // 1. cheap pre-filter: raw swaps(id) → decode → match lp == you + status != 0
  // 2. only then the expensive enriched read (oracle + settle preview)
}
```

Each candidate is **cheap-pre-filtered** on the raw `swaps(id)` record — decode it,
keep only rows where `lp == you` and `status != 0` — *before* paying for the enriched
`getProtectionStatus`, which touches the oracle and computes a settlement preview. An unreadable id is skipped, not fatal: a single bad row
cannot break the whole scan. The two underwriter slices
reuse the same idea — `mm.getBook(owner, { fromSwapId: 1n })` walks the same range
internally.

The three slices are independent React-Query subscriptions, so a degraded depositor read
never blanks your LP protections, and vice-versa. A single
`invalidateAll()` re-fetches everything after any write succeeds.

<Card title="Why a scan, not the subgraph?" icon="magnifying-glass">
The subgraph is the right tool for *unbounded history and aggregates*; it is not yet
indexed (events have been emitted on-chain since deploy block `274081134`). Rather than
block the portfolio behind an offline indexer, the Dashboard reads the chain directly for
the recent, actionable window — the swaps you can still settle or manage. See
[the subgraph integration](/integrate/subgraph) and [what's live](/status).
</Card>

## Section 1 — Protections (LP, claim A)

Each discovered protection is enriched into a `ProtectionStatus` and paired with its
checkpointed fees, then rendered as a card with its **own** settle transaction state — so
each row settles independently.

A protection card surfaces the load-bearing facts of the hedge: the cap and the
[no-bad-debt](/concepts/no-bad-debt) framing. The section note restates the **qualified**
claim verbatim:

> *LPs are always paid — no bad debt in FULL (capped payout + solvent USDC +
> oracle/settlement liveness). Payout = `min(realized IL, MaxIL)`; the cap is load-bearing.*

The payout an expired protection pays is exactly:

$$
\text{payout} \;=\; \min\!\bigl(\text{realized\_IL},\; \text{MaxIL}\bigr)
\;\le\; \text{collateral} \;=\; \text{MaxIL}
$$

which is why the cap is what makes the guarantee hold by construction. See the
[IL formula](/math/il-formula) and the [MaxIL derivation](/math/maxil-derivation).

### Settling at expiry

The **Settle** button on each card calls `sdk.lp.settle(swapId)`.
The SDK recovers the swap's expiry, walks the Chainlink feed for the round bracketing the
expiry timestamp if you pass no hint, then submits `settle(swapId, hintRoundId)`. The
button runs through [`useTx`](/sdk/getting-started) — `signing → pending → success` — and
trims viem's verbose revert dumps to the first line.

<Note>
**Settlement is rail-agnostic and identical to [`/protect`](/app/protect).** Whether the
counterparty is the [cvAMM pool](/protocol/path-a-cvamm) (Path A) or an
[MM](/protocol/path-b-mm) (Path B), `settle` pays your realized IL — **capped at MaxIL** —
from the underwriter's collateral, and returns your NFT. The settlement core does not
branch on the rail; see [the lifecycle](/protocol/lifecycle).
</Note>

## Section 2 — Deposits (depositor, claim B)

This slice reads `depositor.getPosition(owner)` and renders a **senior** and a **junior**
card side by side, each with **Your NAV** (your shares converted to assets via
`convertToAssets`), **Shares**, any queued withdrawal with an unlock countdown, and inline
withdraw controls. The junior card is badged
**FIRST-LOSS**.

The section note states the risk before any number:

> *You underwrite pooled in-range IL via the dual-tranche cvAMM vault. Capital is NOT
> guaranteed — junior is first-loss; senior is protected from underwriting loss only, not
> the systemic tail.*

This is **never merged** with the LP no-bad-debt claim. They are two separate claims:
[claim A](/concepts/no-bad-debt) is a guarantee to the *LP being paid*; claim B is the
*depositor's* capital being at risk. The senior tranche's structural protection is
junior-first only:

$$
\text{senior protected from underwriting loss while}\quad
\text{totalLocked} \;\le\; \text{juniorAssets}
$$

Senior still takes the **systemic tail** (USDC depeg, oracle/settlement failure, contract
bug) once the junior buffer is exhausted. See [tranches](/concepts/tranches) and the
[collateral models](/protocol/collateral-models).

### Withdrawing a tranche

Withdrawals are **two-step and cooldown-gated**, exposed inline on each card:

<Steps>
<Step title="Request withdrawal">
The **Request withdrawal** button queues your full share balance via
`sdk.depositor.requestWithdrawal(tranche, shares)`. It
appears only while `shares > 0n`.
</Step>
<Step title="Wait out the cooldown">
A queued withdrawal shows the share count and an **Unlocks in** countdown. The **Withdraw** button stays disabled until the request
has matured — `withdraw.secondsRemaining === 0n`.
</Step>
<Step title="Withdraw">
Once matured, **Withdraw** calls `sdk.depositor.withdraw(tranche)`.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Limit — a junior withdraw can revert `JuniorBelowLocked`.**
**Why** — junior is the first-loss buffer; the vault will not let a junior exit drop
`juniorAssets` below `totalLocked`, because that would silently strip the senior tranche's
underwriting-loss protection (`totalLocked ≤ juniorAssets`).
**Mitigation** — the revert is surfaced cleanly as an error `WriteResult` on the card,
never a silent failure; it clears as locked collateral is
released by settlements, or you can exit when utilization is lower. This is the protection
working as designed. See [tranches](/concepts/tranches) and the
[invariants](/security/invariants) (I5: `locked ≤ deposited`).
</Warning>

## Section 3 — MM Book (claim B)

The underwriter slice combines two reads: `mm.getMmCollateral(owner)` for the collateral
card and `mm.getBook(owner, { fromSwapId: 1n })` for your active fills. The section note restates the risk:

> *Active swaps you underwrite (Path B) + your collateral usage. MM capital is NOT
> guaranteed — you are short the capped IL claim and post collateral == MaxIL per fill.*

### Collateral card

A degraded read or an address with no record renders a `PendingNote` inviting a deposit;
otherwise the card shows three stats:

| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Deposited** | total dUSDC posted to the `UnderwriterVault` |
| **Locked** (amber) | `Σ MaxIL` across your active fills — your committed collateral; sub-labelled **I5: locked ≤ deposited** |
| **Available** (teal) | free capacity you can still quote against |

The invariant on the card label is [I5 — vault solvency](/security/invariants):
`locked ≤ deposited`, enforced per-MM on-chain. Each Path-B fill locks **exactly MaxIL**
of your own collateral — that is what keeps `payout ≤ collateral` true for the swaps you
write, the [no-bad-debt math](/math/no-bad-debt-math) applied to a single underwriter.

### Active fills

When you have open fills, a panel headers the count and your aggregate exposure — **Σ V0**
and **Σ MaxIL** (amber) — over a per-swap table of swap / V0 / MaxIL / premium / expiry. Discovery is the same on-chain scan, so the **aggregate
exposure is exact** for the scanned window. To manage outstanding *quotes* (cancel a nonce
so it can never fill), go to [`/underwrite`](/app/underwrite) — `cancelNonces` lives there.

<Warning>
**Limit — fill-to-quote attribution is coarse on-chain.**
**Why** — the `SwapCreated` event carries the `mm` address but **not** the `quoteId` or
`nonce`, and a cancelled bit and a filled bit are indistinguishable on the bitmap nonce —
so an on-chain reader cannot join a fill back to the specific signed quote it came from.
**Mitigation** — the panel says so explicitly, and your **aggregate** exposure is still
exact (it is read from the swap records themselves). Precise per-quote attribution arrives
with the `QuoteFilled`-indexing subgraph, whose event is already emitted on-chain. See [the subgraph](/integrate/subgraph) and
[Path B](/protocol/path-b-mm).
</Warning>

## The two limits to read honestly

The Dashboard is fully live within two well-understood bounds. Neither is hidden; both are
named on the page with their reason and path-forward.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="The on-chain scan is bounded to the last 400 swaps">
**Limit.** LP discovery and the MM book walk only the most recent `SCAN_LIMIT = 400` swap
ids. A protection older than that window will not
appear here.

**Why.** With no subgraph yet, each id costs RPC reads (a cheap pre-filter plus, on a
match, an enriched oracle-backed status). An unbounded scan would be slow and could
rate-limit the public RPC; bounding it keeps the portfolio responsive and is more than
enough to cover the actionable window — the swaps near or past expiry that you can still
settle or manage.

**Mitigation.** The section renders an explicit `PendingNote`: *"Discovered by a bounded
on-chain scan of the last 400 swaps. Full position history (and exact uncollected fees)
goes live with the subgraph; the on-chain scan works now"*.
Full, unbounded history is exactly what the [subgraph](/integrate/subgraph) will serve once
indexed. For the current cohort (a single fresh redeploy on 2026-06-05) every live swap is
inside the window.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="getClaimableFees under-states uncollected LP fees">
**Limit.** The fees shown on an LP protection card come from `getClaimableFees`, which
reads the position's **checkpointed `tokensOwed` only** — so it **under-states** the true
uncollected fee balance.

**Why.** Fees accrued since the last on-chain checkpoint live in the pool's per-position
fee-growth accumulators and are not materialized into `tokensOwed` until a `collect`/poke
touches the position. Reading the exact live figure would require replaying fee-growth
math off-chain.

**Mitigation.** The card frames this as a *floor*, not the answer, and the pending note
points to the subgraph for the exact figure. Critically, this is a **display-only**
caveat on a secondary number — it does **not** affect your IL payout. Your hedge payout is
computed from `min(realized_IL, MaxIL)` at settlement and is unaffected by fee display. Note also that the LP keeps **all** position fee accrual
throughout the hedge — Inflexion never custodies the fee stream
(see [the lifecycle](/protocol/lifecycle)).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

and their *path-forward*. The full catalogue lives at

</Tip>

## Quick reference

| Section | Role / claim | Source read | Per-card write |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protections | LP / claim A | scan → `lp.getProtectionStatus` + `lp.getClaimableFees` | `lp.settle(swapId)` |
| Deposits | Depositor / claim B | `depositor.getPosition` | `depositor.requestWithdrawal`, `depositor.withdraw` |
| MM Book | Market maker / claim B | `mm.getBook` + `mm.getMmCollateral` | (cancel nonces on [`/underwrite`](/app/underwrite)) |

All discovery is a bounded on-chain swap scan (`SCAN_LIMIT = 400`), live today; precise
history, aggregates, and exact fees follow the [subgraph](/integrate/subgraph).
