The SDK is not neutral plumbing. It carries the protocol's load-bearing claims **in its
types and exported constants**, so that any UI, bot, or integration that reads from it
inherits the correct framing for free — and so that getting the framing wrong takes
deliberate effort, not just inattention.

This page is the "say it right" reference. Every rule below is something `@inflexion/sdk`
encodes structurally. If you are building on Inflexion, treat these five rules as
non-negotiable in your copy and your tooltips.

<Note>
The framing rules are also captured in code as a self-contained guardrail block: the
no-bad-debt clause, claims A and B (exported verbatim as `LP_CLAIM_A` / `DEPOSITOR_CLAIM_B`),
and the hedge caveat. When in doubt, quote the exported constant rather than paraphrasing.
</Note>

## Rule 1 — In-range convexity hedge, not "IL insurance"

Inflexion lets an LP transfer the **in-range** impermanent-loss risk of a specific
position. Two structural facts make this a convexity hedge rather than insurance, and the
SDK enforces both:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Entry requires Pa ≤ P0 ≤ Pb">
    Out-of-range positions are rejected at creation. The SDK computes
    `inRange = sqrtP0 >= sqrtPa && sqrtP0 <= sqrtPb` in `resolveGeometry`
    and surfaces it on every `LpGeometry` and `EligiblePosition`. This mirrors the on-chain
    `createSwap` in-range gate — there is no way to protect a position that is already out
    of range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payout is min(realized_IL, MaxIL)">
    The settlement payoff is the **capped** IL, not the raw IL. The cap is applied in
    `getPayoffCurve` and in `ilToDate`, where `capHit = payout < il` tells you whether the
    cap bound.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The capped payoff is what makes this *convexity*: the LP is long gamma / long vega on the
in-range region, and the protocol is short it. The cap is the **unit of risk** as well as
the safety mechanism — see [Rule 4](#rule-4-an-mm-quote-is-per-market-never-per-nft).

The realized-IL payoff, with the cap that is load-bearing for everything downstream:

$$
\text{payout} \;=\; \min\!\bigl(\,\text{realized\_IL},\; \text{MaxIL}\,\bigr),
\qquad
\text{realized\_IL} \;=\;
\begin{cases}
V_{\text{hold}} - V_{\text{lp}} & \text{if } V_{\text{hold}} > V_{\text{lp}} \\[2pt]
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

The right branch is invariant **I3** (non-negativity / no underflow) and **I4** (the LP
never profits from the swap): if the LP would have done better holding LP than holding the
underlying, payout is exactly zero. The SDK never produces a negative payout; the
`Priceable<...>` envelope returns `il` and `payout` as unsigned `bigint`.

<Warning>
**Do not call it "IL insurance".** "Insurance" implies an uncapped indemnity against any
loss. Inflexion is an *in-range convexity hedge*: the protection only exists while
$P_a \le P_0 \le P_b$, and the payout is capped at MaxIL. The cap is not a deductible — it
is the mechanism that makes the no-bad-debt guarantee exact (see
[Rule 2](#rule-2-qualified-no-bad-debt-claim-a)). Removing or mis-describing the cap
breaks the framing of the entire protocol.
</Warning>

See [the in-range convexity hedge](/concepts/in-range-convexity-hedge) and
[the IL formula](/math/il-formula) for the full derivation.

## Rule 2 — Qualified no-bad-debt (Claim A)

The LP-facing claim is: **LPs are always paid — no bad debt in FULL mode (invariant I1)**.
The SDK carries this on the LP surface as `noBadDebtFull: true` on `ProtectionStatus` and
exports the full sentence verbatim as `LP_CLAIM_A`.

But the claim is **exact only under its full qualifying clause**. State it any other way
and it is wrong.

<ClaimA />

The clause, spelled out — every term is load-bearing:

| Condition | Why it is required |
| --- | --- |
| **FULL collateralization** | Collateral is locked at MaxIL at creation; `collateral == MaxIL` is invariant **I1**. PARTIAL mode is gated and not live in v1. |
| **Capped payoff** | `payout ≤ MaxIL == collateral` only holds because the payoff is capped ([Rule 1](#rule-1-in-range-convexity-hedge-not-il-insurance)). An uncapped payoff could exceed the posted collateral. |
| **Solvent USDC** | The numéraire (dUSDC, 6-dec) must hold its value. A depeg is a systemic tail the on-chain guarantee does not cover. |
| **Oracle / settlement liveness** | `settle()` must be able to read a price within the liveness window (Fork 1, invariant I8). The SDK's `settle` walks Chainlink rounds to find the bracketing round. |
| **No rehypothecation breach** | Locked collateral must stay instantly liquid — never routed to utilization-gated venues. This is an operational invariant, not an automatic one. |

<Warning>
**Never state no-bad-debt unqualified.** Writing "Inflexion can't produce bad debt" or
"LPs can't lose" without the clause is a defect. It is exact *only* under FULL
collateralization + capped payoff + solvent USDC + oracle/settlement liveness + no
rehypothecation breach. Use the `<ClaimA />` snippet rather than re-wording, and quote
`LP_CLAIM_A` if you need the canonical sentence in code.
</Warning>

<Note>
Claim A is about the **LP being paid**. It says nothing about depositor or MM capital —
that is a separate claim ([Rule 3](#rule-3-capital-is-not-guaranteed-claim-b)). The SDK
keeps them on separate surfaces precisely so they cannot be accidentally merged.
</Note>

See [no-bad-debt](/concepts/no-bad-debt), [the no-bad-debt math](/math/no-bad-debt-math),
and [the invariants](/security/invariants).

## Rule 3 — Capital is not guaranteed (Claim B)

The underwriter-facing claim is the mirror image of Claim A, and it must never be softened.
The SDK stamps `capitalNotGuaranteed: true` on **every** `VaultState` and
`DepositorPosition` result and exports the full sentence as `DEPOSITOR_CLAIM_B`. It is
structurally impossible to read vault state from the SDK without the flag attached.

<ClaimB />

### Both tranches carry risk — differently

The protocol underwrites with a two-tranche waterfall. Neither tranche is risk-free:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Junior — first-loss">
    Junior is the **first-loss buffer**: it captures most of the premium and absorbs
    underwriting losses first. The on-chain `withdraw` enforces the `JuniorBelowLocked`
    gate so junior cannot drain below the locked amount and break senior protection. A
    junior withdrawal that would cross the locked floor reverts and surfaces as
    `WriteResult { status: 'error' }`, not a silent partial.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Senior — protected, not risk-free">
    Senior is structurally protected from **underwriting** loss only — the
    `totalLocked ≤ juniorAssets` junior-first waterfall (invariant I5). It is **not**
    protected from a *systemic tail*: a USDC depeg,
    an oracle/settlement failure, or a contract bug can still impair senior capital. "Protected"
    is a relative, structural statement — never "safe" or "guaranteed".
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The structural fact, as an invariant:

$$
\text{totalLocked} \;\le\; \text{juniorAssets}
\qquad\text{(junior absorbs underwriting loss before senior)}
$$

<Warning>
**Never imply either tranche is risk-free.** Junior is first-loss; senior is protected
from *underwriting* loss while junior buffers, but takes the systemic tail. Render the
`<ClaimB />` disclosure on every depositor-facing surface, and do not borrow Claim A's
"no bad debt" language to describe depositor capital — that conflates two distinct claims.
</Warning>

See [tranches](/concepts/tranches), [the vaults](/protocol/vaults), and
[the risk disclosure](/risk-disclosure).

## Rule 4 — An MM quote is per-MARKET, never per-NFT

This is the rule integrators most often get wrong, because intuition says "I'm pricing
*this* position." You are not. MaxIL is **both** the load-bearing payout cap **and** the
unit of risk:

- MaxIL is **pure geometry** — a function of $(P_a, P_b, P_0)$, frozen at creation.
- It is **L-independent in the fair-rate sense**: the liquidity $L$ scales the dollar
  amount but not the fair *rate*.

Because the unit of risk is geometry, not the specific NFT, **positions are fungible to an
underwriter within a market**. So a market-maker quotes a *market*, not a token:

$$
\text{MM quote} \;=\;
\underbrace{\text{loadBps}}_{\text{a load over on-chain FairPremium}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{[\,\text{minMaxILRatioBps},\, \text{maxMaxILRatioBps}\,]}_{\text{a MaxIL-ratio band}}
\;+\;
\underbrace{\text{maxNotionalV0}}_{\text{capacity}}
$$

The proof that this is per-market and not per-NFT is in the wire format: the
`SignedQuote` struct carries **no `tokenId`**. Its fields are
`mm`, `marketId`, `loadBps`, `minMaxILRatioBps`, `maxMaxILRatioBps`, `quotePrice`,
`priceBandBps`, `model`, `partialRatioBps`, `maxNotionalV0`, `validUntil`, `quoteId`,
`nonce` — geometry-independent, every one.

The SDK leans into this: the first-class streamable signal is `getPoolLoadToBeat` /
`streamPoolLoadToBeat` — the pure pool load an MM must undercut, computed **without any
position geometry**:

```ts filename="mm-quote-per-market.ts"
import { createInflexionSdk, encodeNonce } from '@inflexion/sdk'

const sdk = createInflexionSdk({ rpcUrl, privateKey: MM_KEY })
const mm = sdk.mm

// The "load to beat" is geometry-free — it needs no tokenId.
const tick = await mm.getPoolLoadToBeat(marketId)          // PoolLoadTick

const quote = {
  mm: MM_ADDRESS,
  marketId,                                                 // ← the market, not a position
  loadBps: Number(tick.totalLoadBps) - 50,                  // undercut the pool floor
  minMaxILRatioBps: 0,
  maxMaxILRatioBps: 10_000,                                 // a MaxIL-ratio BAND, not one position's MaxIL
  quotePrice: liveOraclePrice,                              // Fork-2 band anchor
  priceBandBps: 50,
  model: 0,                                                 // FULL (the only v1 mode)
  partialRatioBps: 0,
  maxNotionalV0: 1_000_000_000_000n,                        // capacity (numéraire raw, 6-dec)
  validUntil: BigInt(Math.floor(Date.now() / 1000) + 30),
  quoteId: ('0x' + 'ab'.repeat(32)) as `0x${string}`,
  nonce: encodeNonce(0n, 0),
  // NOTE: there is deliberately no `tokenId` field. A quote prices a MARKET.
}
const signed = await mm.signQuote(MM_KEY, quote)            // enforces I10 + below-pool
```

<Tip>
A quote's `[minMaxILRatioBps, maxMaxILRatioBps]` band says "I will underwrite any
position in this market whose MaxIL ratio falls in this band, up to `maxNotionalV0` of
notional." One signed quote can fill many different NFTs. This is the fungibility that
makes a competitive MM market possible at all.
</Tip>

<Warning>
**Do not describe an MM quote as pricing a specific position.** It prices a market: a
load, a MaxIL-ratio band, and a capacity. If your UI shows "MM quote for token #3218",
that is wrong framing — show "MM quote for market `<marketId>`, valid for any position in
the MaxIL band." The absence of `tokenId` in `SignedQuote` is the proof.
</Warning>

See [MaxIL](/concepts/maxil), [the MaxIL derivation](/math/maxil-derivation),
[Path B](/protocol/path-b-mm), and [quote signing](/integrate/quote-signing).

## Rule 5 — The Phi-sum is read on-chain, never reimplemented

FairValue is an **exact closed-form $\Phi$-sum** — the Stylus `FairValueOracle`
(`fairPremium` / `fairRate`). It is the protocol's single source of pricing truth, and the
SDK **reads** it; it never recomputes it off-chain.

The on-chain read, in the SDK's hot path:

```ts
// Path A — the load-bearing on-chain read. The Φ-sum is NEVER reimplemented off-chain.
const [premium, fairRateWad, sigmaRefWad] =
  await fairValueOracle.read.fairPremium([token, aWad, bWad, durationSeconds, maxIL])
```

### The one permitted duplication — and why it is permitted

There is exactly **one** piece of pricing math the SDK mirrors off-chain: the
deterministic, `pure` **load stack** (`baseLoad` + `utilSkew` + `dispSkew`, clamped to
`maxLoadBps`). It lives in the `cvamm` namespace and is **parity-locked byte-equal** to the
deployed `CvammPricing` Solidity by a dedicated parity test.

Why mirror it at all? Because the deployed `CvammPricing` library is **delegatecall-only**:
a direct `eth_call` to `libs.cvammPricing` reverts (Solidity guards deployed libraries —
confirmed on the 2026-06-05 deploy). The library runs on-chain only via the core's
delegatecall during pricing, so there is no callable on-chain entrypoint to read the load
from. The parity-locked TS port is the only way to preview the load without sending a
transaction.

The split is precise:

$$
\underbrace{\text{premium}}_{\text{Path A}}
\;=\;
\underbrace{\text{fairPremium}}_{\text{read on-chain (Φ-sum)}}
\times
\bigl(1 + \underbrace{\text{totalLoad}}_{\text{mirrored off-chain (load stack)}}\bigr),
\quad
\text{capped at MaxIL}
$$

```ts filename="premium-the-split.ts"
import { cvamm } from '@inflexion/sdk'

// 1. fairPremium + fairRate + sigmaRef come from the on-chain Φ-sum (read, never recomputed).
const [fairPremium, fairRateWad, sigmaRefWad] =
  await fairValueOracle.read.fairPremium([token, aWad, bWad, durationSeconds, maxIL])

// 2. ONLY the deterministic load stack is evaluated client-side via the parity-locked port.
const totalLoad = cvamm.totalLoadWad(sigmaRefWad, util, conc, params)
const premiumA  = cvamm.premiumFromLoad(fairPremium, totalLoad)   // ceil(fairPremium·(1+totalLoad)), capped at MaxIL
//                = exactly `_pricePathAFromFair` on-chain
```

<Warning>
**Never reimplement the $\Phi$-sum off-chain.** Do not "approximate fair value" in JS,
cache a stale `fairRate`, or invert it to back out an implied vol for pricing. The
`FairValueOracle` is the only authority; the SDK reads it fresh every preview. The only
math you may evaluate off-chain is the deterministic load stack — and only via the
parity-locked `cvamm` port, never a hand-rolled copy. If `cvamm` and the deployed library
ever disagree, the parity test fails the build.
</Warning>

<Note>
Inverting `fairRate` to infer implied volatility is also a **circular** signal — it would
just recover the σ_ref the oracle was fed. It is deliberately excluded from the data-moat
signals for exactly that reason. See
[circular vs behavioral](/moat/circular-vs-behavioral).
</Note>

See [the three pillars](/concepts/three-pillars),
[the Phi-sum](/math/fair-value-phi-sum), [the pricing stack](/protocol/pricing-stack),
and [the cvAMM port](/sdk/cvamm-port).

## One more guardrail: the hedge caveat

If you surface the SDK's read-only analytics (`GreeksEngine` / `HedgeSuggester`), there is
a sixth framing rule you must carry verbatim. Every `HedgeSuggestion` attaches
`HEDGE_CAVEAT`:

- The hedge is **approximate** relative to the fixed-maturity IL claim (mismatched expiry,
  funding, discretization).
- It is the **MM's own residual-risk choice**, not a protocol requirement.
- It is **not relied upon for pool solvency** — invariant **I1** (no bad debt, FULL) is
  structural and oracle-independent, fully collateralized at MaxIL **regardless of whether
  the MM hedges at all**.

`executeOnPanoptic` always returns `executed: false` — the SDK never submits a hedge tx.
It is analytics, not execution.

<Warning>
Do not imply the hedge is what keeps the pool solvent. Solvency comes from full
collateralization at MaxIL (I1), which holds whether or not any MM hedges. The hedge is the
MM's private business decision about its own residual risk. Attach `HEDGE_CAVEAT`
verbatim to any suggestion you display.
</Warning>

See [greeks & hedge](/sdk/greeks-hedge) and [the trust model](/security/trust-model).

## Quick checklist for integrators

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Copy that mentions LP protection">
    Say "in-range convexity hedge", not "IL insurance". State the payout as
    $\min(\text{realized\_IL}, \text{MaxIL})$ and note the cap. Mention the entry gate
    $P_a \le P_0 \le P_b$ if there is room.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Copy that mentions no-bad-debt">
    Always attach the full clause via `<ClaimA />` (FULL + capped payoff + solvent USDC +
    oracle/settlement liveness + no rehypothecation breach). Never the bare phrase. This is
    the LP claim — keep it off depositor surfaces.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Copy that mentions depositors or MMs">
    Render `<ClaimB />`. Both tranches carry risk: junior first-loss, senior protected from
    underwriting loss only and exposed to the systemic tail. Never "risk-free" or
    "guaranteed". Do not reuse Claim A's no-bad-debt language here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Copy that mentions MM quotes">
    A quote is per-market: load + MaxIL-ratio band + capacity. The `SignedQuote` has no
    `tokenId`. Never present a quote as pricing one specific NFT.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Anything that computes or displays a premium">
    Read `fairPremium` / `fairRate` from the on-chain `FairValueOracle` fresh. Only the
    load stack may be evaluated off-chain, and only via the parity-locked `cvamm` port.
    Never reimplement or invert the $\Phi$-sum.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Anything that shows history or aggregates">
    History surfaces (load-surface history, quote competition, NAV, net gamma) are
    subgraph-pending. The SDK returns a typed `ApiPending` envelope naming the future
    route — render the pending state, never an error, never fabricated numbers.

  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
