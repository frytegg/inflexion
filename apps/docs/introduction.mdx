**Inflexion is the first market for Uniswap v3 impermanent-loss risk — and the first to price that risk on-chain.**

A Uniswap v3 LP brings a specific position (its NFT), picks a duration, and pays a fixed upfront **premium**. At expiry the protocol pays the LP their realized impermanent loss — **capped at MaxIL** — trustlessly, from collateral that an underwriter locked up front. The product is precisely an [**in-range convexity hedge**](/concepts/in-range-convexity-hedge), not "IL insurance": the cap is load-bearing, not cosmetic.

<Note>
The payoff is $\min(\text{realized\_IL},\ \text{MaxIL})$. Entry requires the position to be in range — $P_a \le P_0 \le P_b$ is [enforced at creation](/concepts/in-range-convexity-hedge) — and the cap is what makes full collateralization (and therefore the [no-bad-debt guarantee](/concepts/no-bad-debt)) possible.
</Note>

## In one paragraph

> If you've ever provided liquidity on Uniswap and ended up with less than if you'd just held — that's impermanent loss. It costs LPs over a billion dollars a year and there's no trustless way to hedge it. Inflexion is the first market that prices that risk on-chain: a pooled underwriter always quotes you a fair, code-capped price, market makers compete to beat it, and if your position suffers impermanent loss within its range you're paid back — from pre-locked collateral, with no middleman who can run off with the money.

## What it is, in one line

> Inflexion is the first market for Uniswap LP convexity priced on-chain — **MaxIL is the capital unit.** A pooled cvAMM always quotes a code-capped fair price with dual-tranche structure (junior vol-seller, senior savings account), and competing market makers undercut it. The resulting flow is a structural DeFi volatility-risk-premium surface for Uniswap LPs — the first on-chain dataset of this market's microstructure.

## Why this market didn't exist before

Uniswap v3 LPs carry a **structural short-gamma exposure** — their position value is a concave function of price, so they lose on both up and down moves relative to holding. Impermanent loss is the realization of that exposure, estimated at **&gt;\$1B/year of realized losses across DeFi**, with no trustless, non-inflationary way to hedge it.

Three things blocked a clean on-chain hedge until now:

## How it works, in five parts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Capped in-range IL transfer">
    Coverage pays $\min(\text{realized\_IL},\ \text{MaxIL})$. [**MaxIL**](/concepts/maxil) is the maximum in-range IL of the *specific* position — pure geometry computable at creation from $(P_a, P_b, L, P_0)$, **frozen at creation**, and **identical across all three durations.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="On-chain fair pricing">
    The protocol computes and publishes $\text{FairPremium} = \text{fairRate} \cdot \text{MaxIL}$ on-chain via the [`FairValueOracle`](/concepts/fair-value) — an exact closed-form [$\Phi$-sum](/math/fair-value-phi-sum), never a fitted surface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Two rails, one settlement core">
    [Path A](/protocol/path-a-cvamm) is the always-on pooled cvAMM (signature-free, on-chain). [Path B](/protocol/path-b-mm) is competing market makers (EIP-712 signed quotes). [`createSwapRouted`](/protocol/routing) gives the LP the **cheaper of `{pool, best MM quote}`.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Full collateralization (FULL mode, the default)">
    The underwriter locks exactly $\text{MaxIL}$. Since $\text{covered\_payoff} \le \text{MaxIL} = \text{collateral}$ by construction, FULL mode **cannot produce bad debt under any price path** — invariant [I1](/security/invariants), provable by Foundry invariant tests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Non-custodial on-chain settlement">
    At expiry, `settle()` reads the Chainlink round pinned at $T$, computes realized IL using the $L$ stored at creation, pays the LP $\min(\text{realized\_IL},\ \text{MaxIL})$ from the underwriter's locked collateral, returns the residual, and returns the NFT.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The three pillars

The protocol stands on [three pillars](/concepts/three-pillars) — read these before anything else.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="On-chain published FairValue" icon="square-root-variable" href="/concepts/fair-value">
    An exact closed-form $\Phi$-sum, evaluated live per quote by the production **Stylus** `FairValueOracle` — **never reimplemented off-chain.** $\text{fairRate}$ carries all the vol/time dependence; MaxIL carries none.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The cvAMM — floor of liquidity" icon="layer-group" href="/protocol/path-a-cvamm">
    An always-on, signature-free pooled underwriter quoting off `FairPremium`, **capped in code at** $\text{FairPremium}\cdot(1+\text{maxLoad})$ by invariant I10. It removes cold-start: there is always a price.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MM competition — ceiling of price" icon="gavel" href="/protocol/path-b-mm">
    Sophisticated MMs stream firm EIP-712 signed quotes (no last-look) and win only by beating the capped pool. Hedged MMs **export** short-gamma risk out of the system.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
**Floor + ceiling in one line:** the cvAMM is the floor of liquidity (always quotes a code-capped price); the MMs are the ceiling of price (win only by beating it). `createSwapRouted` routes to the cheaper rail and never reverts on a bad MM quote — it falls back to the pool, and only the executed rail mutates nonce, capacity, and lock.
</Tip>

## MaxIL — the cap and the unit of risk

MaxIL is two things at once, and both are load-bearing:

- **The cap.** $\text{IL}(P)$ is convex on $[P_a, P_b]$, so its in-range maximum is at a boundary: $\text{MaxIL} = \max\!\big(\text{IL}(P_a),\, \text{IL}(P_b)\big)$. Because $\text{collateral} = \text{MaxIL} \ge \text{payout}$, no bad debt in FULL is a *construction*, not a hope.
- **The unit.** MaxIL is pure geometry, frozen at creation, L-independent in the fair-rate sense — which makes positions **fungible to an underwriter within a market.** An MM quote is therefore [**per-market**](/protocol/markets) (a load + a MaxIL-ratio band + capacity), **never per-NFT** — the signed quote carries no `tokenId`.

See [MaxIL — the cap and the unit of risk](/concepts/maxil) for the full doctrine, and [the worked example](/math/worked-example) for one position priced across three durations on a single, constant MaxIL.

## The honest line

Two claims, always separate, never merged:

1. **LPs are always paid** — no bad debt in FULL, code-enforced by invariant [I1](/security/invariants).
2. **Depositors and MMs can lose principal** — the pool is a volatility seller, and capital is **not** guaranteed for either tranche (junior is first-loss; senior is protected from *underwriting* loss only, never from the systemic tail).

<ClaimA />

It is **not** Bancor (no token inflation), **not** GammaSwap (no active management), **not** Panoptic (the LP pays one number), and **not** "insurance" (it is a collateralized derivative). See [what this is not](/what-this-is-not) for the full set of differentiators.
