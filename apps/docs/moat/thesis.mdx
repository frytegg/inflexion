## The thesis in one paragraph

The order flow Inflexion generates is a byproduct that is itself a product: **the
first structured view into the microstructure of the DeFi LP volatility-risk
premium**. No venue today
prices the *in-range convexity of a specific Uniswap v3 range*. Inflexion does,
on-chain — and every fill records **who paid what load, over a transparent
realized-vol reference, for exactly which position geometry, at which maturity**. The
aggregate of those choices is a dataset that **cannot exist anywhere else**, because
no other system is the mechanism that creates it. The data is built passively from
day one and exposed as **free public APIs** (The Graph subgraph + REST) — **data is
the moat, not a paywall; revenue is protocol fees**.

<Info>
**Two sentences, both load-bearing, kept separate.** *Revenue is fees.* The protocol
earns from the spread it intermediates, not from selling data. *Data is the moat.*
The defensibility is not access control — the APIs are free and public — it is that
Inflexion is the only place this market clears, so it is the only place the dataset
accrues. A competitor cannot buy the dataset; they would have to build the mechanism
that creates it.
</Info>

### Limit → Why → Mitigation

The honest framing decomposes into one structural limit with a clear cause and a clear
path forward:

<Warning>
**Limit.** At launch — one market maker, a handful of fills — the data is the
**architecture and the first view**, not a mature dataset. The *dynamic* signals (the
pool-vs-MM spread read as forward vol; bucketed load *dispersion* across MMs) are
single-point, not distributions.

**Why.** A spread needs two sides and a distribution needs many draws. With one MM you
observe *one* undercut, which conflates "the market expects vol to fall" with "this MM
has cheaper hedges" — two orthogonal drivers that cannot be separated until competing
MMs exist. And there is no historical realized-IL dataset to calibrate against, *because the
protocol is the mechanism that creates it*.

**Mitigation / path forward.** Every primitive is captured from day one so the
dynamics are *reconstructable as volume arrives, never retroactively lost*. The
on-chain moat events (`SwapPriced`, `QuoteFilled`) are **live now** and stamp the
dataset's genesis block; the off-chain telemetry sinks (`DEMAND_LOG`,
`COMPETITION_LOG`) are **capturing from the first interaction** — and that latent /
dynamic data is *unreconstructable retroactively* by [invariant I7](/security/invariants),
which is exactly why it is logged now. The
structures sharpen into dynamics with `≥3` competing MMs and `~30–50` fills per bucket
— weeks-to-months at launch flow, not a redesign.
</Warning>

## The implied-vol-inversion idea was dropped as circular

The moat's credibility rests on **not being circular**. An earlier draft framed the
product as "infer implied volatility by inverting `fairRate`". **That idea is DROPPED
as circular** — and we explain *why* on purpose, because
demonstrating the rigor is itself part of the moat.

Every premium in the protocol is, by construction:

$$
\frac{\text{charged premium}}{\text{MaxIL}} \;=\; \text{fairRate}(\sigma_{\text{ref}})\,\bigl(1 + \text{load}\bigr)
$$

Here `fairRate` is the protocol's *own* published closed form — [the Phi-sum](/math/fair-value-phi-sum),
computed by the Stylus `FairValueOracle` — and `σ_ref` is the protocol's *own*
published realized-vol oracle. So inverting `fairRate` from the charged premium:

$$
\sigma_{\text{implied}} \;=\; \text{fairRate}^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\text{charged}}{\text{MaxIL}\,(1+\text{load})}\right) \;=\; \sigma_{\text{ref}}
$$

recovers **nothing but our own published `σ_ref`** (plus our own dealer load stack).
It tells an outside observer of our oracle exactly what they already knew. That is not
a market-implied vol — **it is a tautology.**

<Note>
**The fix — reframe the moat around behavior, not derivation.** A number is a signal
only if it comes from a free CHOICE the protocol does *not* compute, **measured
against** the transparent mechanical baseline, never *derived from* it. The behavioral
degrees of freedom are the MM's freely chosen `loadBps` (Path B) and the LP's
geometry/duration choice. The full circular-vs-behavioral treatment, signal by signal,
lives at [/moat/circular-vs-behavioral](/moat/circular-vs-behavioral).
</Note>

The non-circularity decision table:

| Source of the number | Circular? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MM-signed `quote.loadBps` (Path B) | **NO** | An external actor's free choice; the protocol does not compute it. Capped only by `loadParams.maxLoadBps` (I10). |
| Which `tokenId` an LP brings + duration chosen | **NO** | LP demand choice; the protocol does not pick the geometry. |
| Path-A pool load `totalLoadWad(σ_ref, util, conc)` | **YES** | Pure deterministic function of protocol state. Inverting it recovers our own formula. Kept as the **mechanical baseline / "price-to-beat"**, not a behavioral signal. |
| `fairRate(a, b, σ_ref, T)` | **YES** | Our own published closed form — the dropped IV-inversion idea. |

**The rule, stated verbatim:** a signal
is non-circular **iff** its informative degrees of freedom come from the MM's
`loadBps` or the LP's geometry/duration choice — measured AGAINST the transparent
mechanical baseline, not derived from it.

## What the dataset actually is

The defensible asset is the join of three things no other system holds together,
atomically, per fill:

1. **The behavioral load** — the markup the actor freely chose above the protocol's
   [published fair value](/concepts/fair-value): the MM's `QuoteFilled.loadBps` on
   Path B (the single non-circular load datum).
2. **Over a transparent reference** — the on-chain realized-vol oracle
   [σ_ref](/math/sigma-ref), stamped on every fill via `SwapPriced.sigmaRefWad`, so
   the load *above* it is a well-defined, identifiable quantity rather than a
   back-solved, non-identifiable "implied σ".
3. **For exactly which geometry** — the specific position's width × distance-to-edge ×
   duration, recovered off-chain by decoding the swap's `tokenId` via
   `NPM.positions(tokenId)` at the `SwapCreated` block, plus `expiry − createdAt`.

[MaxIL](/concepts/maxil) is what makes this legible: because premium is quoted as a
percent of MaxIL, positions are **fungible** to an underwriter and the load is
**range-agnostic** (an MM quote is per-MARKET — a load + a MaxIL-ratio band +
capacity — never per-NFT). That is why bucketing the load by geometry is *informative*
rather than noisy.

### The five behavioral signals (lead with the verdicts)

The moat is **five non-circular behavioral signals**, each with an explicit
circularity verdict. Lead with the verdicts — they are the rigor. The deep treatment
is at [/moat/five-signals](/moat/five-signals).

| # | Signal | Behavioral source | Non-circular? |
| - | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Realized clearing **load** over a transparent `σ_ref` (bucketed by geometry) | MM `loadBps` vs the pool baseline | **PARTIAL — Path B only** (Path-A pool load is mechanical/circular; keep it as the price-to-beat) |
| 2 | Pool-vs-MM load **spread** + MM win-rate / win-depth | `poolLoad − mmLoad` (the subtraction removes the circular leg) | **YES — as a spread** |
| 3 | **Term structure** of convexity (`loadBps` slope across 7/30/90d per range) | MM `loadBps` slope by duration | **PARTIAL** (Path-A load is duration-independent by construction → flat; the slope is an MM signal) |
| 4 | Moneyness / **demand skew** by geometry | which `tokenId`/duration LPs bring (realized) + unfilled interest (latent) | **PARTIAL** (realized = LP choice → non-circular; latent half is off-chain) |
| 5 | Net convexity / **gamma supply** (protocol-wide) | `Σ` gamma over the active set | **YES — as a supply/quantity gauge** |

<Warning>
**Two structural exclusions that travel with every load signal.** (1) The clearing
load is *CONTAMINATED* by liquidity, smart-contract-risk, capital-lock, and
inventory-skew premia — **trade the SPREAD (MM − pool), not the level**. (2) **Exclude cap-bound fills**
(`SwapPriced.cappedAtMaxIL == true`) from Signals 1, 2, and 3 — when `premium ==
MaxIL` the load was truncated by the cap, so the number you observe is the cap, not the
actor's load.
</Warning>
